## Outreach plan — first 10 churches

1. **Frontier Church** (Mark's own, already the live test case). Get the QR code printed and in use on a Sunday. Real usage before any cold outreach.
2. **Ask the pastor for a 2-sentence testimonial** after 2-3 weeks of real use. This becomes the core of all marketing.
3. **5 nearby Grand Rapids churches** — personal visits or warm intros, not cold DMs. Offer to set up their QR code on the spot (takes 5 minutes).
4. **Michigan church networks** — Classis/regional gatherings where pastors swap tool recommendations.
5. Post the launch posts above only after the Tend Facebook page exists and at least one real church is live.
