Monday. The people who trusted you with something vulnerable never know if

## Outreach rules
- Warm intros first, then the lead list. Never cold-call a Sunday morning.
- One church live and happy beats ten signed up and silent. Ask every pilot
  pastor for a two-sentence testimonial after three weeks.
- Do not mention SMS or text-in until Twilio compliance clears.
- Do not promise features marked "(planned)" on the pricing page.
